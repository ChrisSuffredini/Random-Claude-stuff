How it works

  • Every CS2-era HLTV result since 2023 feeds a Glicko rating per team on a per-map scale, one over all matches and one over LAN only. Rating uncertainty grows with inactivity and after lineup changes.
  • Each team's features (both ratings, HLTV ranking points, roster continuity, recent form, player ratings of the current five) go through a small neural net and a logistic model to give one strength number; the ensemble averages them with Glicko.
  • Stages are simulated with Valve's Swiss rules: 1v9 opening round, Buchholz-then-seed pairing, no rematches, Bo1 with Bo3 deciders. Each simulated Major gives every team a shared "form" shock so results correlate.
  • The Pick'Em card is searched to maximise the chance of 5+ correct over the simulated outcomes.
  • CS is noisy, Bo1s especially. Even a perfect model misses often; use the probabilities, not just the picks.
